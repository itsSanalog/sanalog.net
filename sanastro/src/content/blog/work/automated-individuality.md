[Marathons are being held 140 years since the first car](/blog/work/anemic-identity/). People are driving to the finish line and celebrating amongst the runners.

***

The surprise: generative AI generates. Machine-made content is beyond abundant—it's not just ubiquitous, it's pervasive.

Whatever. The situation is something I am somewhat indifferent to. Such detachment comes not out of my principles, but more from the fact that it is something beyond my control. The idea of growing pain tolerance isn't new.

Obviously however, it sucks to have to review a commit with ten thousand additions and a few hundred deletions. Or when someone responds to your question with a machine answer as if I couldn't ask ChatGPT myself. Enough people interface with enough AI that more users are slowly straying from it. Platforms seem to be recognising that we would rather not consume AI slop. There are movements towards <a href="https://gruhn.me/blog/2026-08-03/" target="_blank" class="extlink">not being a meat proxy</a>. The hot trend is to acquire taste.

That's great! So what the fuck are these guys doing, trying that to automate that process?

### We can tell that your website was vibe coded.

The obvious silicon-valley engineer response to that fact was to analyse and <a href="https://impeccable.style/slop/" target="_blank" class="extlink">create a tally</a> so it could be automatically detected and naturally, automatically removed. There literally is a skill called `taste-skill`. Similar things happen in the backend with documentation and docstrings, etc.

These cheap remedies will just iterative upon themselves. Good code can and are written with AI, but good developers make sure they are used within a tight, controlled scope. Traditional iterative processes are still valuable—no competent developer expects to one-shot a high-quality product. To many non-developers trying to make their app or website for cheap, such workflows are generally outside their expertise.

### We can tell your images were generated.

Of course there are band-aid solutions in this domain as well, such as <a href="https://www.recraft.ai" target="_blank" class="extlink">Recraft</a>. We know it wouldn't work. Let's move on.

On services offered by OpenAI or Google, people have noticed that photorealistic styles output images that are more passable than your average generation. Results improve as you specify lighting, texture, perspective, lens effects, shadows, white balance, vignettes...

> Ultra-realistic cinematic portrait of a stylish man drinking coffee in a retro bathroom interior. Camera placed very close above eye level, extreme wide-angle lens (16-20mm feel), strong perspective distortion, slightly tilted framing for dynamic composition. Subject looking directly into the lens while lifting a turquoise ceramic cup to his lips.

You see, it helps if the prompter has prior knowledge of such photography terms, or a solid grasp of the intended aesthetic direction. As it currently stands, reliable and effective desloppification still involves a certain level of expertise and intent.

### We can tell your LinkedIn posts are written by Claude

~~Good, LinkedIn can disappear.~~

Just to put the examples out there: <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing" target="_blank" class="extlink">Wikipedia list of signs of AI writing</a>, and the inevitable AI skills that try.

A better solution is to fine-tune a model yourself, using something like Gemma 4, trained on 'good' writing. Yield best results by feeding it your own words. Again, this requires you to have written in the first place. Desloppification requires expertise, intent, and effort.

***

I like to think 'taste' as a by-product of expertise, intent, and effort, which are themselves a reflection of one's desires, values, and character.

People want taste mostly for others to see them as tasteful. The easy way to do so is to deslop AI slop, aka 'larp'. But one needs taste to deslop AI slop while said taste only comes from having solid principles and ethics. By larping with AI you refute such ideologies so how could you even begin to covet taste without the ethos to incubate it? It's a pointless exercise.
